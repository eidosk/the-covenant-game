A tiny puzzle-platformer where a lone horse from Noah's Ark finds its horn and becomes a unicorn, tasked with bringing the very first rainbow into the world across 13 levels. Inspired by Sokoban puzzle games.


 Controls                                                                                                                                                                                          

- Arrow keys: move
- Space: fires your horn's beam
- Z: undo the last move
- R: restart the current level
